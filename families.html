一个真实故事（脱敏）A real story, anonymized

从“我不知道我喜欢什么”，到第一次愿意展示自己的作品From "I do not know what I like" to showing work with ownership.

一位学生刚开始时对未来很抗拒，问到喜欢什么只说“不知道”。前几周没有急着做难项目，而是先从他愿意聊的日常观察切入，把一个很小的情绪陪伴想法变成网页原型。第一次看到自己的页面能被别人打开后，他开始主动提出功能、修改文案、练习路演。到后期，作品不只是申请材料里的一个链接，而是他重新确认“我能做成一件事”的证据。One student began with strong resistance toward the future and answered "I don't know" whenever asked about interests. The first weeks did not force a hard project; we started from an everyday observation he was willing to discuss and turned a small emotional-support idea into a web prototype. Once he saw others could open his page, he began proposing features, editing copy and practicing the demo. By the end, the project was not just a link in an application file. It became evidence that he could make something real.

最后确认几个边界A few final boundaries.

费用怎么沟通？How is pricing discussed?

费用不在网页公开展示，请私联 Becky。通常会先做一次评估，确认孩子是否适合、适合标准还是深度陪跑，再沟通具体方案与费用。Pricing is not listed publicly. Please contact Becky directly. We usually begin with an assessment, confirm fit and track level, then discuss the specific plan and pricing.

什么情况适合开始？When is this a good fit?

适合有潜力但卡住、仍有一点兴趣火花、家长愿意配合的孩子。ADHD / 神经多样性孩子也可以评估，因为重点不是逼孩子更用力，而是找到低压力入口。It fits capable students who are stuck, still have a small spark of interest, and whose parents are willing to collaborate. ADHD and neurodivergent learners can also be assessed because the goal is not to push harder, but to find a low-pressure entry.

什么情况暂时不适合？When is it not a fit yet?

如果目标只是短期提分、代写作业、完全不愿参与，或期待“包过”承诺，就不适合这个项目。这里做的是真实能力和真实作品，不是包装。It is not a fit if the goal is short-term score boosting, ghostwriting, complete non-participation, or guaranteed admission promises. This program builds real ability and real work, not packaging.

不会编程 / 家长不懂 AI 怎么办？What if the child or parent does not know coding or AI?

不需要提前会，从零开始带。标准节奏通常是每周 1 次 1v1，课后留出项目推进时间；家长只需要参与评估与关键反馈。No prior skill is needed. We start from zero. The standard rhythm is usually one 1-on-1 session per week, with project time between sessions; parents join assessment and key feedback moments.
