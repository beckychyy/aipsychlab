合作流程 · 基于 MTSS Tier 1 全体支持标准
Partnership Process · MTSS Tier 1 Universal Support
01
学校需求深度评估
School Needs Deep Assessment
与学校管理层进行深度访谈，了解学生群体构成、现有心理支持系统、教师团队状态与家长社群特点，产出《学校心理生态诊断报告》。
In-depth interviews with school leadership to understand student demographics, existing psychological support systems, teacher team dynamics, and parent community characteristics — producing a School Psychological Ecosystem Diagnostic Report.
02
定制课程体系设计
Customized Curriculum Design
基于诊断报告，为学校量身设计涵盖学生课程、教师培训与家长讲座的三方系统方案，确保课程内容与学校文化高度融合。
Based on the diagnostic report, designing a three-tier system covering student curriculum, teacher training, and parent lectures — ensuring curriculum content is deeply integrated with the school's culture.
03
驻校落地执行
On-Site Implementation
AI Psych Lab团队进驻学校，完成课程教学、工作坊带领与教师培训。全程可结合PsychLab.AI Bot为学生提供课后情绪追踪支持。
AI Psych Lab team on-site for curriculum delivery, workshop facilitation, and teacher training. PsychLab.AI Bot can be integrated for post-session emotional tracking support for students.
04
效果追踪与持续迭代
Outcome Tracking & Continuous Iteration
项目结束后持续追踪学校心理健康指标变化，产出《年度效果研究报告》，作为学校心理教育体系的长期数据支撑。
Post-project ongoing tracking of school mental health indicators, producing an Annual Outcome Research Report as long-term data support for the school's psychological education system.
