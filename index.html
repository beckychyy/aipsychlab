从一把黄椅子
到 AI Psych Lab

From a Yellow Chair
to AI Psych Lab

2023年，在哈佛创新实验室的支持下，Becky 把一把黄色的椅子搬进了中国几所高中的走廊。那个项目叫黄椅子——不评判，不给答案，只是坐下来，陪着。这是她用最朴素的方式做的心理公益，并经媒体报道传播。

黄椅子在中国持续落地，但与此同时，她的博士研究让她看到了一件更大的事——在研究中国家庭养育与AI时代儿童发展的过程中，越来越多的父母找到她，问她同样的问题。

AI的出现，让她看到了一种新的可能：不是用技术替代陪伴，而是用科学放大陪伴的精准度。

于是，AI Psych Lab 从黄椅子里生长出来——在哈佛创新实验室持续孵化，由一位创始人与一群实验室伙伴共同推动，服务于在AI时代里寻找方向的中国家庭与学校。

In 2023, with the support of Harvard Innovation Labs, Becky carried a yellow chair into the corridors of several high schools in China. The project, called Yellow Chair, was simple: sit down, listen, and be present — no judgment, no prescribed answers. This grassroots mental health initiative was reported in the media and continued to grow.

As Yellow Chair took root, Becky's doctoral research was revealing something larger. While studying Chinese family parenting and child development in the AI era, more and more parents were finding her — asking the same questions.

The rise of AI opened a new possibility: not using technology to replace human connection, but using science to amplify its precision.

And so, AI Psych Lab grew from that yellow chair — incubated at Harvard Innovation Labs, driven by one founder and a team of lab members, serving families and schools seeking direction in the AI era.
