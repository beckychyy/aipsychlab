评估会看什么What it checks

不是问“孩子好不好”，而是判断下一步从哪里开始Not whether the student is good enough, but where the next step should begin.

01

孩子状态Student state

内驱力、沟通、屏幕、焦虑、兴趣是否还在，以及是否有 ADHD / 神经多样性相关信号。Drive, communication, screen use, anxiety, remaining interests and ADHD / neurodiversity signals.

02

AI 进入点AI entry point

孩子是否适合从网页、AI 陪伴、游戏、影像、学习工具或公益议题切入，先做出一个能打开的作品。Whether to begin with web apps, AI companion products, games, media, learning tools or impact projects.

03

陪跑强度Mentorship intensity

初步判断适合轻评估、标准 1v1，还是需要更深度的状态支持、作品路径和家长沟通。Whether the family needs a light assessment, standard 1-on-1, or deeper support across state, products and parent communication.
