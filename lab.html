四大研究方向Research Directions

每个方向，都要落到产品与教育现场Every direction lands in product and education practice.

核心方向：执行功能、内在 vs 外在动机、兴趣驱动神经可塑性。产品落地：为 1v1 学生建立内驱力画像与项目选题路径。Executive function, intrinsic vs extrinsic motivation and interest-driven neuroplasticity. Product use: drive maps and project selection.

核心方向：前额叶与情绪调节、压力反应、ADHD/ASD 神经多样性、AI 时代数字焦虑。产品落地：学习状态调节与压力支持策略。Prefrontal regulation, stress response, ADHD/ASD neurodiversity and digital anxiety. Product use: state regulation and stress support.

核心方向：认知与学习风格评估、NLP 伦理边界、人机协作思维。产品落地：AI Lab 课程、学生项目工作流与作品集系统。Learning-style assessment, NLP ethics and human-AI collaboration. Product use: AI Lab curriculum and student project workflows.

核心方向：依附理论、父母焦虑代际传递、跨文化养育，是前三大方向的底层基础。产品落地：亲子沟通与家庭协作策略。Attachment, parental anxiety transmission and cross-cultural parenting as the foundation. Product use: family communication and collaboration.
