Research Areas

四大研究方向Four Research Areas

→ 支柱一：内驱力→ Pillar 1: Intrinsic Drive
内驱力神经科学Motivational Neuroscience
+
如何科学识别与激活孩子真正的内在动力内核与执行功能
Scientifically identifying and activating each child's true intrinsic motivation core
基于约翰斯·霍普金斯大学神经教育研究，探索动机、奖励回路与认知投入之间的神经机制。核心研究方向：
  • 执行功能与自我调节的神经发育路径
  • 内在动机 vs. 外在奖励的脑区激活差异
  • 兴趣驱动型学习的神经可塑性机制
  • 目标设定与前额叶皮层功能的关系
产品落地：《动力地图报告》评估框架 · 家庭未来力实验室核心评估工具
Drawing on JHU neuroeducation research, exploring neural mechanisms between motivation, reward circuitry, and cognitive engagement.Core Research Areas:
  • Neural developmental pathways of executive function and self-regulation
  • Brain activation differences: intrinsic vs. extrinsic motivation
  • Neuroplasticity mechanisms in interest-driven learning
  • Goal-setting and prefrontal cortex function
Product Application:Motivation Mapping Report · Family Future Readiness Lab core assessment tools
→ 支柱二：情绪管理→ Pillar 2: Emotional Regulation
情绪神经科学与韧性发展Emotional Neuroscience & Resilience
+
脑科学视角下的情绪调节机制与AI时代心理韧性培养
Neuroscience-based emotional regulation and resilience-building in the AI era
聚焦青少年情绪调节的神经发育机制，积累1000+小时一线临床数据。核心研究方向：
  • 前额叶皮层与情绪调节系统的协同发育
  • 神经多样性青少年的情绪模式（ADHD、自闭症谱系）
  • 压力神经生物学与创伤知情干预方案
  • AI时代数字焦虑与情绪边界建立
产品落地：家庭未来力实验室情绪韧性模块 · PsychLab.AI Bot情绪支持算法
Focusing on neurodevelopmental mechanisms of adolescent emotional regulation. Backed by 1,000+ clinical hours.Core Research Areas:
  • Co-development of prefrontal cortex and emotional regulation systems
  • Emotional patterns in neurodiverse youth (ADHD, ASD)
  • Stress neurobiology and trauma-informed intervention
  • Digital anxiety and emotional boundary-setting in the AI era
Product Application:Emotional resilience module · PsychLab.AI Bot emotional support algorithms
→ 支柱三：AI竞争力→ Pillar 3: AI Competitiveness
AI辅助个性化教育技术AI-Assisted Personalized Education
+
用AI实现真正个性化的教育心理评估与成长路径设计
Using AI for truly personalized educational psychology assessment and growth design
探索心理学测评与AI技术结合，为每个孩子生成专属成长数据画像。核心研究方向：
  • AI辅助认知模式与学习风格评估系统
  • 自然语言处理在心理支持对话中的伦理边界
  • 人机协作思维模式与批判性思维培养
  • 数字心理健康：屏幕时间与前额叶发育
  • AI时代的创造力保护与人类独特性
产品落地：PsychLab.AI Bot对话逻辑 · 未来心智计划课程框架
Exploring how psychological assessment and AI combine to generate individualized growth profiles.Core Research Areas:
  • AI-assisted cognitive pattern and learning style assessment
  • Ethical boundaries of NLP in psychological support dialogue
  • Human-AI collaborative thinking and critical thinking
  • Digital mental health: screen time and prefrontal development
  • Protecting creativity and human uniqueness in the AI era
Product Application:PsychLab.AI Bot dialogue logic · Future Mind Program curriculum framework
✦ 三大支柱的底层基础✦ Foundation for All Three Pillars
家庭系统与养育环境Family Systems & Parenting Environment
+
依附理论与家庭系统治疗：孩子成长生态系统的底层逻辑
Attachment theory and family systems therapy: the foundational logic of child development
基于Bowlby依附理论与Bowen家庭系统治疗理论，贯穿所有产品的底层理论基础。核心研究方向：
  • 安全型依附关系对执行功能的长期神经影响
  • 父母焦虑传递机制与代际创伤干预
  • 高净值家庭特有的教育压力模式与应对
  • 跨文化家庭养育差异（中美教育价值观比较）
  • 单亲、留守、异国他乡家庭的特殊支持需求
产品落地：所有产品均含家庭系统评估维度 · 家长coaching是每个层级的必要组成
Drawing on Bowlby's attachment theory and Bowen family systems therapy — underlying foundation across all products.Core Research Areas:
  • Long-term neural impact of secure attachment on executive function
  • Parental anxiety transmission and intergenerational trauma intervention
  • Educational pressure patterns in high-net-worth families
  • Cross-cultural parenting differences (China-US comparison)
  • Special support needs for single-parent and overseas families
Product Application:Family systems assessment across all products · Parent coaching integral at every tier
Methodology

MTSS 方法论框架MTSS Methodological Framework

所有产品均基于 MTSS 多层次支持体系（Multi-Tiered System of Supports）——国际循证心理教育领域最权威的干预框架，由哈佛与约翰霍普金斯大学研究团队持续验证。
