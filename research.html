Research Areas

四大研究方向Four Research Areas

→ 支柱一：内驱力→ Pillar 1: Intrinsic Drive
内驱力神经科学Motivational Neuroscience
+
如何科学识别与激活孩子真正的内在动力内核与执行功能
Scientifically identifying and activating each child's true intrinsic motivation core
基于约翰斯·霍普金斯大学神经教育研究，探索动机、奖励回路与认知投入之间的神经机制。

核心研究方向：
· 执行功能与自我调节的神经发育路径
· 内在动机 vs. 外在奖励的脑区激活差异
· 兴趣驱动型学习的神经可塑性机制
· 目标设定与前额叶皮层功能的关系

研究成果直接应用于《动力地图报告》的评估框架设计与家庭未来力实验室的核心评估工具。
Drawing on JHU neuroeducation research, exploring neural mechanisms between motivation, reward circuitry, and cognitive engagement.

Core Research Areas:
· Neural developmental pathways of executive function and self-regulation
· Brain activation differences between intrinsic vs. extrinsic motivation
· Neuroplasticity mechanisms in interest-driven learning
· Goal-setting and prefrontal cortex function

Findings directly inform the Motivation Mapping Report assessment framework and the Family Future Readiness Lab's core evaluation tools.
→ 支柱二：情绪管理→ Pillar 2: Emotional Regulation
情绪神经科学与韧性发展Emotional Neuroscience & Resilience
+
脑科学视角下的情绪调节机制，与AI时代高压环境下的心理韧性培养
Neuroscience-based emotional regulation mechanisms and resilience-building in the high-pressure AI era
聚焦青少年情绪调节的神经发育机制，研究如何在AI时代高压环境中建立可持续的韧性系统，积累1000+小时一线临床数据。

核心研究方向：
· 前额叶皮层与情绪调节系统的协同发育
· 神经多样性青少年的情绪模式识别（ADHD、自闭症谱系）
· 压力神经生物学与创伤知情干预方案
· AI时代数字焦虑与情绪边界建立

研究成果直接指导家庭未来力实验室的情绪韧性评估模块与PsychLab.AI Bot的情绪支持算法。
Focusing on neurodevelopmental mechanisms of adolescent emotional regulation and sustainable resilience systems in high-pressure AI-era environments. Backed by 1,000+ hours of clinical data.

Core Research Areas:
· Co-development of prefrontal cortex and emotional regulation systems
· Emotional pattern recognition in neurodiverse youth (ADHD, ASD)
· Stress neurobiology and trauma-informed intervention
· Digital anxiety and emotional boundary-setting in the AI era

Findings directly guide the emotional resilience assessment module and PsychLab.AI Bot's emotional support algorithms.
→ 支柱三：AI竞争力→ Pillar 3: AI Competitiveness
AI辅助个性化教育技术AI-Assisted Personalized Education
+
用AI技术实现真正个性化的教育心理评估与成长路径设计
Using AI to deliver truly personalized educational psychology assessment and growth pathway design
探索心理学测评与AI技术结合，为每个孩子生成专属的成长数据画像，这也是PsychLab.AI Bot的核心研究基础。

核心研究方向：
· AI辅助认知模式与学习风格评估系统
· 自然语言处理在心理支持对话中的伦理边界
· 人机协作思维模式与批判性思维培养
· 数字心理健康：屏幕时间与前额叶发育的关系
· AI时代的创造力保护与人类独特性培养

研究直接支撑PsychLab.AI Bot的对话逻辑设计与未来心智计划的课程框架。
Exploring how psychological assessment and AI combine to generate individualized growth data profiles — forming the core research foundation for PsychLab.AI Bot.

Core Research Areas:
· AI-assisted cognitive pattern and learning style assessment systems
· Ethical boundaries of NLP in psychological support dialogue
· Human-AI collaborative thinking and critical thinking cultivation
· Digital mental health: screen time and prefrontal cortex development
· Protecting creativity and human uniqueness in the AI era

Research directly supports PsychLab.AI Bot's dialogue logic and the Future Mind Program's curriculum framework.
✦ 三大支柱的底层基础✦ Foundation for All Three Pillars
家庭系统与养育环境Family Systems & Parenting Environment
+
依附理论与家庭系统治疗：孩子成长生态系统的底层逻辑
Attachment theory and family systems therapy: the foundational logic of the child development ecosystem
基于Bowlby依附理论与Bowen家庭系统治疗理论，研究家庭生态如何影响孩子的内驱力、情绪调节与AI时代适应能力。这是贯穿所有产品的底层理论基础。

核心研究方向：
· 安全型依附关系对执行功能的长期神经影响
· 父母焦虑传递机制与代际创伤干预
· 高净值家庭特有的教育压力模式与应对
· 跨文化家庭养育差异（中美教育价值观比较）
· 单亲、留守、异国他乡家庭的特殊支持需求

所有产品设计均将家庭系统纳入核心评估维度，家长coaching是每个服务层级的必要组成。
Drawing on Bowlby's attachment theory and Bowen family systems therapy, researching how family ecosystems shape children's intrinsic drive, emotional regulation, and AI-era adaptability — the underlying theoretical foundation across all products.

Core Research Areas:
· Long-term neural impact of secure attachment on executive function
· Parental anxiety transmission and intergenerational trauma intervention
· Unique educational pressure patterns in high-net-worth families
· Cross-cultural parenting differences (China-US educational value comparison)
· Special support needs for single-parent, left-behind, and overseas families

All products incorporate family systems as a core assessment dimension; parent coaching is integral at every service tier.
Methodology

MTSS 方法论框架MTSS Methodological Framework

所有产品均基于 MTSS 多层次支持体系（Multi-Tiered System of Supports）——国际循证心理教育领域最权威的干预框架，由哈佛与约翰霍普金斯大学研究团队持续验证。

Tier 3 is the highest-intensity level — smallest reach, greatest depth. The Family Future Readiness Lab uses research-grade assessment and long-term growth plans, ensuring every child's support is genuinely bespoke, never generic.
