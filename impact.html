"Starting from a yellow chair, we have always believed —
mental health is not a privilege, it is every child's birthright."

AI Psych Lab 将商业实践与公益行动系统性绑定。这不是附加的CSR，而是我们从第一天起就写进基因里的承诺：用我们能做到的方式，让更多孩子被看见、被支持。

AI Psych Lab systematically binds commercial practice with social impact. This is not add-on CSR — it's a commitment written into our DNA from day one: using what we can do to help more children be seen and supported.

研究桥梁计划
Research Bridge Initiative
Harvard Education Research · China Bridge

作为哈佛教育学院的全球校友与在读研究员，Becky 持续在国际顶尖教育研究与中国本土教育现场之间搭建桥梁。

目前，已有多位哈佛教育学院的研究团队联系 Becky，希望借助她的网络与资源，在中国真实的教育场景中收集研究数据——让国际研究不再停留于理论，真正扎根中国教育语境。

这个项目的意义在于双向流动：让中国孩子的真实成长数据被国际研究看见，也让国际最前沿的教育心理研究成果反哺中国家庭。

As a Harvard HGSE global alumna and current researcher, Becky continuously bridges international education research and China's real educational landscape.

Multiple Harvard HGSE research teams have reached out to Becky to leverage her network and resources for collecting research data within authentic Chinese educational contexts — allowing international research to move beyond theory and genuinely take root in China.

This project moves in both directions: making the real developmental data of Chinese children visible to international research, while channeling cutting-edge educational psychology findings back to Chinese families.

一校一善
One School, One Good
Yunnan Rural Education Initiative

AI Psych Lab 做出一个承诺：每完成一个学校合作项目，将把该项目收入的一部分，专项捐助至云南山区小学的教育建设。

捐助内容涵盖：学习材料与文具采购、心理健康启蒙课程开发与落地、基础教育资源扩充，以及教师能力建设支持。每一笔捐助都会持续追踪使用情况，并在官网公示。

我们相信，当我们帮助城市里有资源的家庭更好地支持孩子时，也有责任让更远处的孩子被看见——让心理健康的启蒙，不只属于少数人。

AI Psych Lab makes a commitment: for every school partnership completed, a portion of the project revenue will be dedicated to educational development at rural primary schools in Yunnan's mountain regions.

Donations cover: learning materials and stationery procurement, development and delivery of mental health literacy curriculum, expansion of basic educational resources, and teacher capacity-building support. Every donation is tracked and publicly disclosed on this website.

We believe that when we help resourced urban families better support their children, we also have a responsibility to make more distant children visible — so that mental health education is not the privilege of the few.
