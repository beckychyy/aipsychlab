Becky 的方法Becky's Method

科学不是冰冷的标签，AI 也不是炫技的工具Science is not a cold label, and AI is not a showpiece.

Becky 相信，真正有效的支持，是先精准理解一个孩子的状态、兴趣和卡点，再用清晰的项目路径让他重新体验“我能做成”。AIPsychLab 的核心不是包装背景，而是让孩子在心理被看见的同时，真正获得未来掌握力。Becky believes support works when we first understand a child's state, interests and stuck points, then design a clear project path where the child experiences "I can make this happen." AIPsychLab is not background packaging; it builds future agency through being understood and creating real work.
