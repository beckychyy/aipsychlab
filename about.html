About · Founder

Becky Chen

JHU 教育博士在读 · Harvard HGSE 校友 · AI Psych Lab 创始人

JHU EdD Candidate · Harvard HGSE Alumna · Founder of AI Psych Lab

📷

becky.jpg
"学术根基 × 临床深度 × 人文温度"
"Academic Rigor × Clinical Depth × Human Warmth"

她是一位令人瞩目的00后。早年曾担任联合国UNICEF咨询顾问，深耕国际公共政策领域；同时身兼企业家助残论坛主席，以行动推动社会融合与包容性发展。

如今，她以哈佛大学教育学院全球校友会主席的身份持续链接全球教育资源，并以约翰霍普金斯大学教育博士研究员的角色深耕学术前沿——研究方向聚焦于中国家庭养育与AI时代儿童心理发展的交叉议题。

她相信，真正的教育变革，不仅需要技术的力量，更需要读懂人心的智慧。这也是 AI Psych Lab 诞生的原点。

A remarkable member of Gen-Z, Becky began her career as a UNICEF Consultant, working at the intersection of international policy and human development. She also served as Chair of the Entrepreneurs for Disability Forum, championing social inclusion and accessibility.

Today, she serves as President of the Harvard HGSE Global Alumni Association and as an EdD Researcher at Johns Hopkins University, where her work focuses on Chinese family parenting and child psychological development in the AI era.

She believes that true educational transformation requires not just technology, but the wisdom to truly understand the human heart — the founding insight of AI Psych Lab.
